Free plan and saved recipes

The free cookbook allows up to 20 saved recipes. If your subscription ends while you have more, your saved recipes remain available; you can manage them but cannot add more until fewer than 20 remain or your subscription resumes. See our pricing FAQs.

You can use the same email for privacy questions and requests relating to data associated with Recipe Assistant. See our data deletion information and Privacy Policy draft.

App-store subscriptions

If a subscription was purchased through Google Play or Apple, the corresponding store manages recurring billing and cancellation. The app store provides purchase receipts and payment support.
