1. About Recipe Assistant

Recipe Assistant is a personal cooking and recipe-management application. You can create, save, edit, import, export and organise recipes, plan meals, prepare shopping lists and use optional AI tools. Some features work locally; cloud AI requests need an internet connection and an eligible account or subscription.

2. AI assistance and safe cooking

AI-generated recipe text, images, ingredient recognition, dietary suggestions and cooking guidance may be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable. Review the details before using them. Confirm ingredients and allergens, dietary restrictions, food freshness and storage, and safe preparation and cooking temperatures yourself. An AI-generated recipe or photograph is not proof that a meal is safe or suitable. The app does not provide medical advice or a professional nutrition consultation.

3. Free cookbook and complimentary AI use

The Free plan permits up to 20 personal saved recipes. You can continue opening, editing, exporting and deleting recipes that you already saved. If your paid subscription ends while you have more than 20 recipes, they are not automatically deleted. You cannot add new recipes on the Free plan while you have 20 or more saved recipes; you can add again after reducing the total below 20 or resubscribing.

Eligible AI tools may include three successful complimentary uses per feature, separate from a paid store trial. These are not a monthly renewal and do not automatically start billing. Usage limits and eligibility are enforced by the service and may vary across supported features. Unsuccessful requests are not intended to consume a successful-use allowance.
