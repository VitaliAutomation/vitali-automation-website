The service

Recipe Assistant provides tools to save, organise, create and import cooking recipes, with optional AI assistance. Some AI features require internet access and a valid entitlement. The current website describes the planned subscription release; the store version available today may differ.

AI output limitations

Generated recipes, ingredient recognition and other AI responses may contain mistakes. Check quantities, dietary requirements, allergens, food storage and cooking temperatures independently. The app is a planning and information tool; it does not replace qualified health, nutrition or food-safety advice.

Subscriptions and complimentary uses

Free cookbook: the Free plan allows up to 20 saved recipes. Existing recipes can still be viewed, cooked from, edited or deleted. If a paid subscription ends while more than 20 recipes are saved, those recipes are not automatically deleted. New recipes cannot be added on Free until the collection contains fewer than 20, or subscription access resumes.

AI access: the new release is planned to offer three successful complimentary uses of each eligible AI feature. Complimentary uses do not automatically enrol users in a subscription. Additional AI access requires an optional paid subscription, subject to stated monthly usage allowances.

Pricing: the planned UK subscription price is £4.99 per month. The actual purchase screen will display the applicable local price, taxes, billing frequency, automatic-renewal terms and cancellation information before confirmation. The subscription model is recurring, not a one-time purchase.

Purchases and cancellation

When offered, subscriptions will be billed and managed through the relevant app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). Users can cancel there. Cancelling a subscription normally stops the next renewal while paid features remain available until the current billing period ends. After access ends, the Free plan's recipe-adding limit applies, without automatic deletion of already saved recipes.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel billing. Refund rights and cancellation rights remain subject to applicable law and the purchasing store's terms.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the service, attempt to bypass access and usage limits, submit content you have no right to use, or interfere with other users or the backend.

Availability and changes

We may improve the features and reliability of Recipe Assistant over time. The final terms will describe how changes affecting purchases, availability and user rights are handled.

Law and business details

Consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected. The final published terms must identify the legal service provider and provide any required business address and contact details, consistent with UK rules and the app stores’ trader declarations. These details are pending verification and have not been invented for this draft.
