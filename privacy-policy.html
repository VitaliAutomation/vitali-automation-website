Privacy Policy

Information about data collected when you use the cookbook and its optional online AI features.

Updated 16 November 2026. This notice relates to Recipe Assistant by Vitali Automation. For privacy enquiries, access, corrections or deletion requests, contact vitaliautomation@gmail.com.

1. What the app does locally

Your personal recipe collection, favourites, meal plans, shopping lists and locally selected recipe photos are primarily held in the app's private storage on your device. Recipe imports, exports or backups that you make or save elsewhere are controlled by you and may remain in the destination you chose. Merely searching your local recipe collection does not upload your complete cookbook.

2. Information used for cloud features

When you elect to use a cloud feature, we may process the recipe text, ingredients, cooking preferences, prompts, selected photos, recognised information, dietary or allergy instructions you submit, AI responses and metadata needed to recover a result. The app asks for agreement before certain AI content is sent. If you decline, the associated cloud operation does not proceed. Do not include unnecessary personal data or other people's sensitive details in images or requests.

We process installation and account references, device/session credentials, consent state, request IDs, feature usage and quota records, timestamps, error/security diagnostics and similar operational data to provide access, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems and avoid duplicate calls or charges.

3. Sign in with Google

For Android automatic cloud registration or existing-account linking, Google identity proof is verified by our Oracle backend. The proof may contain Google account claims such as your name and email address, even though the app does not use those fields as a public profile. The backend uses a pseudonymous binding to recognise the same Google identity, preserve access and protect quotas and purchases. Your identity provider, Google, separately processes sign-in according to its own terms.

4. Processing providers and international transfers

The Recipe Assistant service is reached through api.vitaliautomation.com and hosted on Oracle Cloud infrastructure. Requested AI text or image operations are processed with OpenAI through that backend. Store purchases, cancellations and entitlement verification involve Google Play or Apple as appropriate. Optional import from web addresses may contact external websites or search providers; on-device text recognition uses Google ML Kit and may produce limited provider diagnostics. The public website uses GitHub Pages; technical access records may be held by the hosting and infrastructure providers. These providers can process data in other countries depending on their services and contractual arrangements. We review appropriate protections under applicable law and do not claim that every service stores information only in the UK.

Processing by service providers may involve destinations outside the United Kingdom. The appropriate transfer mechanism depends on the provider, destination and applicable law, potentially including a UK adequacy regulation or an appropriate contractual safeguard. For questions about particular recipients or the safeguards applicable to your information, contact us at vitaliautomation@gmail.com. The exact provider agreements and transfer arrangements must be maintained and verified by the operator; no claim is made that every provider processes data exclusively in the UK.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Payments are completed through the relevant app store; we do not receive your full payment-card details. We may receive and retain transaction identifiers, order or subscription state, encrypted purchase proof, period information and a pseudonymous installation/account link needed to verify, restore and secure paid access, detect fraud and meet relevant record-keeping duties.

6. Lawful grounds for using information (United Kingdom)

Where UK data-protection law applies, the legal ground depends on the activity. We process the information necessary to supply a requested app/account feature or verify an optional paid subscription to perform the relevant service contract or steps requested before it. For free services and related technical operations, we may rely on legitimate interests in reliably operating and protecting the service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. We use relevant information to meet any applicable legal obligations, including necessary billing or compliance records. We handle support enquiries based on the circumstances, which may include performance of a contract or our legitimate interests in providing support.

When you voluntarily submit allergy or dietary-health information as part of an optional AI request, the app asks for affirmative agreement before that information is sent. If it constitutes special-category information, explicit consent is the additional condition we rely upon for that optional processing where required by UK law. You can withdraw the agreement for future AI processing, but it does not reverse operations already performed. Do not submit medical records or other information that is unnecessary for your cooking request.

Our legitimate interests include detecting abuse, securing authentication, preventing trial or purchase fraud, investigating service failures, and keeping necessary operational and audit records. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests using the contact details above; we will consider your request under applicable law.

7. Retention and security

Local recipes stay on your device until you remove them, clear the app's local data or restore/change the device; user-created backups can remain separately. Cloud account/session and quota records are kept while needed for operation, access, fraud prevention or applicable legal requirements. Recoverable cloud AI results and usable credentials can be removed through the verified operator-assisted process described in Data deletion. Limited non-content usage, billing, security and erasure-audit records may remain for legitimate obligations. Operational backups and service-provider copies may have different lifetimes; a deletion request does not guarantee immediate removal of every independently stored or provider-controlled copy. We do not publish an unverified universal deletion deadline.

Retention is assessed by the purpose of each record: cloud access and recoverable results while needed to deliver and recover the requested service; quota and session-integrity records to prevent repeat trials or fraud; purchase and dispute evidence for entitlement checks and applicable records duties; security logs to investigate abuse or faults; and support correspondence until the request is resolved and any relevant follow-up or legal obligations have ended. Backups follow separate recovery cycles. You can ask for the criteria that apply to your request, and for deletion of data that no longer has a lawful purpose, using the contact above.

Service controls include device-bound authorisation, protected administration and restricted backend access. No system is completely risk-free; report suspected privacy or security issues to the contact above.

8. Choices and rights

You may manage or delete local recipes and export content using the app's available functions. Where required by law, you may ask us to access, correct, restrict or delete personal data, or object to certain processing. We may need sufficient information to identify your installation or account securely. Email vitaliautomation@gmail.com; do not send passwords, ID tokens, MFA codes or payment card numbers. You may withdraw applicable AI processing agreement in the app. Withdrawal does not automatically remove content that has already been processed; the separate deletion request procedure applies.

Where applicable you may also request data portability and withdraw consent for consent-based processing. You have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. These rights depend on the data and the legal basis and may be subject to lawful exceptions.

9. Children, health and food safety

Recipe Assistant is a general-audience cooking app, not an advertising or social-profiling service. Users may voluntarily enter allergies or dietary-health preferences; these may be shared with the chosen AI service only when the relevant cloud feature is requested and consented to. Avoid submitting medical records or unnecessary sensitive data. AI suggestions cannot replace professional medical or food-safety guidance. The app is not intended to collect children's details for advertising.

10. Website and policy updates

The Vitali Automation website is a public information site and does not intentionally include advertising tracking scripts or a public customer sign-in system. We may revise this notice when the app, backend, legal requirements or processing partners change. The date at the top indicates the latest version. Our Terms of Use, deletion information and support page contain related information.
