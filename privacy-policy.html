Privacy Policy

A working draft for the upcoming Recipe Assistant AI release. Current published-app terms may differ.

Draft for internal review — not final legal advice.This page describes the planned new AI architecture. Before removing the site's noindex setting or linking it from the apps, verify retention periods, actual deletion mechanics, billing flows, legal identity/contact requirements, and each store's current policies.

Version: Draft 1 — 8 November 2026. Contact: vitaliautomation@gmail.com.

Who provides the app?

Recipe Assistant is developed by Vitali Automation. Additional business/contact details required for the final published notice will be confirmed before launch.

What information is used?

The app may process recipes and cooking preferences you enter, photographs you choose to submit for recipe or kitchen recognition, requests and replies needed for AI features, installation/device identifiers used to enforce access, and technical usage records used for limits, reliability and misuse prevention. Subscription status and purchase verification may be processed when relevant to your access.

What happens when you request AI features?

AI requests are sent to the Recipe Assistant backend hosted on Oracle Cloud and may be processed by OpenAI to provide the requested text or image result. Avoid including information about other people or sensitive personal details in prompts or photos you upload. The final policy must accurately identify any other processors and data flows used by the released app.

Local and cloud data

The app supports local recipe storage and user-selected file export/import. Some cloud processing records, approved food images, usage accounting and installation status exist on the server. The final policy will specify what is stored in each location, how long it is retained and what users can delete or request to have deleted.

Payments

Where subscriptions are offered through Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms manage purchases and payment methods under their own policies. Recipe Assistant may receive purchase verification and entitlement information, rather than card payment details.

Public website

This static website does not intentionally include analytics trackers, advertising scripts or an account sign-in system. The hosting provider may process technical connection and security logs when you access it.

Your choices and requests

Contact vitaliautomation@gmail.com about privacy, correction or deletion questions. We will verify the request and explain what data can be actioned under the implemented system and applicable law. See data deletion.

Children and safety

The released app's age rating, any age-related access restrictions and processing provisions will be described consistently with its store listings and actual product implementation before publication.

Changes to this notice

Updates to features, providers or subscription processing may require changes to this policy. The final published document will show an effective date and updated contact information.
