Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can I use Recipe Assistant without paying?

Yes. The free cookbook allows up to 20 saved recipes, and you can still view, use, edit and delete the recipes in your collection. Eligible AI features also have a limited number of successful complimentary uses.

Will my 200 recipes disappear if I cancel?

No. Your saved recipes are not automatically removed when subscription access ends. Even if 200 recipes remain on the device, you can still use and manage them. You cannot add more on Free until fewer than 20 remain, unless you subscribe again.

If I delete recipes after cancelling, when can I add another?

Once your collection contains fewer than 20 recipes, you can add another until it reaches 20 again. If it contains 20 or more, adding is blocked on Free; existing recipes remain accessible.

Does a complimentary AI request start a paid subscription?

No. There is no automatic conversion to a paid subscription. A paid purchase must be actively confirmed through the relevant app store.

How many complimentary requests do I get?

The upcoming release is planned to offer three successful complimentary uses of each eligible AI feature. If an operation fails, it should not consume a successful-use allowance.

How do I cancel?

Manage your subscription through Google Play or Apple, depending on where you bought it. Cancelling stops future renewals according to the store terms, while access normally continues through the paid period. Deleting the app alone does not cancel the subscription.

Can AI answers be wrong?

Yes. Check ingredients, allergens, quantities, food storage and cooking temperatures independently, especially when preparing food for other people.
