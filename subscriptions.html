Common questions

Good to know.

Does using complimentary AI requests start a paid subscription?

No. Complimentary uses are separate from any recurring subscription. A purchase must be confirmed through the relevant store.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Google Play or Apple are generally managed through that store's subscriptions settings. Cancelling stops future renewals under the store's applicable rules; uninstalling the app alone does not cancel billing.

What about existing Lifetime Pro purchases?

Existing purchases and entitlements must be honoured according to the terms under which they were sold. Details of the transition will be confirmed before the subscription version launches.

Can AI answers be wrong?

Yes. Generated recipes and safety advice may be inaccurate. Always check allergens, ingredients, safe cooking temperatures and other important instructions.
