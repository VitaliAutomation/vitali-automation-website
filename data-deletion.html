Data deletion

How to ask about Recipe Assistant information stored on your device or processed by its cloud services.

Draft for internal review — not final legal advice.This page describes the planned new AI architecture. Before removing the site's noindex setting or linking it from the apps, verify retention periods, actual deletion mechanics, billing flows, legal identity/contact requirements, and each store's current policies.

Recipes stored on your device

Recipes saved in Recipe Assistant are managed on the device. A change from a paid subscription to Free does not automatically delete any saved recipes, even when more than 20 are present. The 20-recipe Free limit affects only whether new recipes may be added.

If you choose to delete a recipe or an exported backup, remember that other copies you made may still exist. The exact removal and restore steps will be verified for the final Android and iOS builds before publication. Uninstalling or resetting a device can remove local data if you have not kept a backup.

Cloud records and AI operations

Recipe Assistant's Oracle backend may hold installation/access information, AI usage accounting and operational records. Some AI processing involves third-party service providers. The final deletion policy will distinguish records that can be immediately removed from those subject to necessary retention obligations.

How to make a request

Email vitaliautomation@gmail.com with the subject Recipe Assistant data request. Tell us what you want to access, correct or delete and what platform you use. Don't send passwords, MFA codes or payment-card numbers. We may need to verify that the request concerns your installation.

What this does not cancel

Deleting an app or requesting data deletion does not itself cancel an app-store subscription. Manage billing through the store where you purchased it.
