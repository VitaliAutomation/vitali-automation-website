Data deletion

Request account or cloud-content deletion and understand what stays on your phone or with an app store.

Updated 16 November 2026. Recipe Assistant is operated by Vitali Automation. Contact vitaliautomation@gmail.com for data and account requests.

Request to delete your Recipe Assistant cloud account and associated personal data: use the in-app request below or email vitaliautomation@gmail.com. You can request account deletion after uninstalling the app; reinstalling is not required. We verify control of the relevant account or installation before acting.

1. Request deletion from the app

When available in your version, open Settings → About / App info → Data deletion and send an authenticated request. The app may return a case or support reference. Receipt of that reference confirms a request was recorded; it does not mean that all deletion steps have already been completed. A verified operator processes the request separately.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the phone or cannot use the in-app option, email vitaliautomation@gmail.com with subject Recipe Assistant data deletion request. Mention your platform and, if available, your non-secret support installation reference. Explain whether you want cloud account access disabled, recoverable AI content deleted, or assistance identifying the affected installation. Do not send passwords, raw Google identity tokens, developer codes, MFA codes or full payment card details. We may ask for proportionate verification before removing records.

3. What the cloud deletion process does

After verification and operator approval, deletion of the identified Recipe Assistant cloud account removes usable sessions and credentials, deletes recoverable AI result content and related stored source material, and ends access to that account. A deletion journal is retained to help prevent previously erased content being restored from older backups. Limited non-content quota and security records, required billing or legal records, and a disabled pseudonymous Google identity binding may be retained where needed for fraud prevention, service integrity or legal obligations. The retained records are not usable to continue the deleted account. We do not represent account deactivation alone as account deletion. Provider-held data, separate support records and off-server backups are assessed through the applicable follow-up process; we do not promise immediate deletion of every independent copy.

4. What is not automatically deleted

Your personal recipes, photos and other local data on the phone are not automatically erased by a cloud deletion request. Delete those in the app, or remove local app data using your device's settings after saving any backups you want to keep. User-created recipe exports and copies outside the app must be removed where you stored them. Approved master-library food photos, external service records and legal billing records are not necessarily deleted as part of an individual cloud erasure.

5. Subscriptions remain separate

Deleting cloud data or uninstalling Recipe Assistant does not cancel your monthly Google Play or Apple subscription. Cancel or manage billing in the app store where the purchase was made.

6. Follow-up and privacy rights

Requests are examined by an authorised person. We will explain any limits or retention obligations relevant to the request and provide an update through the available support channel. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

If the request cannot be identified or verified from the available non-secret information, we will explain what further information is needed instead of deleting another user's records. We will respond within the period required by applicable data-protection law, including explaining any lawful exception or justified extension. The status of an in-app case is not itself proof that erasure has finished.
